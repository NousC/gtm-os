# The GTM Playbook: the Dream 1000 loop

The Agentic GTM System is the architecture. This is the motion that runs on top of it: the end-to-end account-based loop. Pick the companies you would love to work with, build the list, score it, learn each prospect's world, write copy that sounds like a peer, send across channels without double-touching, and let every reply make the system smarter.

The principle the whole loop turns on: **the resolved record is the brain, and the tools are dumb pipes.** Every stage writes back into one record. Nothing lives in a single tool in isolation.

---

## The loop in one picture

```
1. FIND         seeds -> lookalike companies -> the decision-maker at each, verified
2. SIGNAL       company signals (the website) + person signals (their posts)
3. SCORE        ICP fit, hybrid: a seed score now, a learned model as deals close
4. PERSONALISE  per-prospect copy from company + person signals, tiered
5. SEND         push to your senders, suppress anyone already in play
6. REPLY        every touch flows back, resolved to one person
7. LEARN        a weekly review sharpens the ICP and the signal weights
```

---

## 1. Find: build the list

Start from a few seed companies you already love, or a description of the niche. Expand to lookalikes, then find the real decision-maker at each one with a verified email. Stamp the source on every lead so you can compare reply rates across sources later. Output is one list, each lead carrying name, email, domain, company, and LinkedIn.

## 2. Signal: two layers, two homes

This is the core of the motion. A company has many people you will reach. The company facts are shared, the personal facts are not.

- **Company signals** come from the website and what the record already knows. They live on the company: stack, hiring, momentum, friction, the domain.
- **Person signals** come from a prospect's recent posts. They live on the person: their intent, their voice, their angle.

A person inherits the company signals and adds their own. So at one account the founder, the head of growth, and the marketing lead all share the company truth but each carries their own voice. Five different emails, one company truth.

## 3. Score: ICP fit, learned over time

Hybrid scoring. Show the seed score (the scan's first read) until the model has trained on real outcomes, then switch to the learned model. Recording a signal re-scores the account automatically. The model sharpens every time a deal closes.

Tiers, with the line drawn at ICP 70:

- **85+**: work by hand. Bespoke everything.
- **70 to 85**: automate. Variable-driven copy.
- **under 70**: drop.

## 4. Personalise: copy that sounds like a peer

Read the person's post signals plus the company signals, then write. The shape is Situation, Insight, Inquisition. Three lines, earn a reply not a meeting, in your own voice (this is where `context/voice-and-tone.md` earns its place).

- **Top tier (85+):** a bespoke sequence written from that person's signals. Fully unique.
- **Mid tier (70 to 85):** one base sequence per angle, with per-prospect variables filled from the signals. Skeleton shared, values unique. This is what scales.

## 5. Send: dumb pipes, the record coordinates

The senders just execute. The record does the four things no sender can:

1. **Route**: pick the channel per lead.
2. **Suppress**: before any push, send only the net-new, never anyone already touched on any channel. This only works because the same person is resolved across channels.
3. **Sequence**: run the cross-channel cadence (no LinkedIn reply, then email).
4. **Track**: every push is tagged so it flows back.

## 6. Reply: everything comes back

Every reply, connection, open, and meeting is ingested and resolved to one person. The status of each prospect is derived from that stream: added to a campaign, then live once sent and opened, then producing once they reply, then done when the stream goes quiet.

## 7. Learn: the weekly review

Each week, per active campaign: pull the sender's totals, read the resolved funnel and the signal and ICP attribution, write a consistent report so weeks compare, and sharpen the ICP and the signal weights from what actually drove replies. The report is platform volume plus "who, why, and which signal won." That last part is the piece no sequencer can give you.

---

## Where this lives in the kit

- **Find, Signal, Score** are the System of Record and Context filling up with real account truth.
- **Personalise, Send** are the System of Actions running on that context.
- **Reply, Learn** are the loop closing, the record getting smarter every cycle.

You do not need to run the full loop on day one. Most operators start with Find and Signal on a single list, then add the rest as the context fills in. The point of the kit is that each stage writes into one record, so the system compounds instead of resetting every campaign.
