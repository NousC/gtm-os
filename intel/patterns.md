# Patterns

The recurring themes that surface across your meetings, your saved sources, and your accounts. The signal under the noise. You add to it any time something clicks, and `/audit` flags it if it drifts.

A pattern is not a single event. It is the third time you noticed the same thing. When a pattern hardens into a choice, log the choice in `intel/decisions/log.md` and link back here.

Format: a short named pattern, what you keep seeing, and what it might mean.
