# Sources

External resources worth keeping: articles, videos, competitor moves, posts, anything that informs what you are building. The outside world, distilled.

One dated file per source or per theme (`0629-growth-unhinged-ai-gtm.md`). Capture the link, then the part that matters: the one or two things you actually want to remember and why they are useful to you. Do not paste the whole article. A source you summarized in three lines is worth more than one you pasted in full and never reread.

When a source changes how you see something, that belongs in a `views/` file. When it triggers a choice, that belongs in `intel/decisions/log.md`. Sources feed both.

Format:

```
# Title of the thing

Link: https://...
Date saved: 2026-06-29

Why it matters: one or two lines on what you took from it and how it applies to you.
```
