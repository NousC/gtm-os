# Meetings

Internal team and co-founder conversations, distilled. One dated file per meeting (`0629-roadmap.md`).

This is for the inside of the company: what you and your team decided, argued about, and need to remember. Customer and prospect meetings do not live here. Those belong in the resolved record (the System of Record), tied to the account. This folder is the internal counterpart.

Capture: who was there, what you talked about, what you decided or left open, and what happens next. The decisions graduate to `intel/decisions/log.md`. The open questions and the recurring themes feed `views/` and `patterns.md`.

If your resolved record holds transcripts of internal calls (because a teammate is in there as an internal-tagged person), `/intel` pulls those and drafts the distilled note here so you are not transcribing by hand.

Format:

```
# Roadmap sync with Akash

Date: 2026-06-29
Present: you, Akash

Talked about: ...
Decided: ...  (log the real ones in intel/decisions/log.md)
Open: ...
Next: ...
```
