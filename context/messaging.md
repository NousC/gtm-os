# Messaging

> How you frame the problem and the proof. The raw material every piece of outreach and content draws from. Filled by `/onboard` from Q4 and your samples. Reads alongside `positioning.md` and `voice-and-tone.md`.

## The problem, in the buyer's words

(How the customer describes the pain before they have ever heard of you. Use their language, not your category language.)

## The way you frame it

(The angle you take on that problem. The reframe that makes your offer the obvious answer.)

## Proof

(The evidence you are real. Results, numbers, named customers, a before-and-after story. Whatever you can stand behind.)

## Objections and answers

(The three or four things a buyer pushes back on, and how you answer each honestly.)

## Message angles

(The two or three distinct hooks you lead with for different segments or signals. The pain-led angle, the value-led angle, the segment-specific angle.)
