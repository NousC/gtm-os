# ICP: Ideal Customer Profile

> The company you sell to and the buyer inside it. Two layers, both matter. Filled by `/onboard` from Q3. This is what every list-build, score, and outreach decision reads.

## The company

- **Industry / category:** (what they do)
- **Size:** (headcount, revenue, or stage band)
- **Signals of fit:** (what a good-fit company looks like: tools they use, how they sell, what they are hiring for, signs of momentum)
- **Disqualifiers:** (what rules a company out, even if it looks close)

## The buyer inside the company

- **Role / title:** (the person you actually sell to)
- **What they own:** (their job, their target, what they are measured on)
- **What they care about:** (the outcome that matters to them)
- **What makes them act:** (the trigger or pain that turns interest into a reply)

## The buying committee

(Beyond the main buyer, who else is in the room: the champion, the blocker, the budget holder. Who you need on side.)

## Tiering

(How you rank fit. A simple version: top-tier accounts you work by hand, mid-tier you automate, the rest you drop. Set the line where it makes sense for you.)
