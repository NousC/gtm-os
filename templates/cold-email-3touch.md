# Cold email, 3-touch sequence

An example template so the folder is not empty. Copy it, change it, delete it. The shape
is a signal-led first touch, a value follow-up, and a soft breakup. Slots in `{braces}`
get filled per prospect from the resolved record and the signal brief, in your voice
(`context/voice-and-tone.md`). Keep each email short. Earn a reply, not a meeting.

---

## Touch 1, day 0 (signal-led)

**Subject:** {3-5 words, lowercase, about them, no pitch}

{One line that names the specific signal, observed not flattered: "saw {signal}".}
{One line connecting that situation to the problem you solve, in their words not yours.}
{One soft question that is easy to answer. No calendar link.}

{first_name_sign_off}

## Touch 2, day 3 (value)

**Subject:** re: {same thread}

{One line of real value tied to their situation: a specific idea, a number, a relevant
example. Not "just bumping this".}
{One line, the same soft ask, reframed.}

## Touch 3, day 7 (breakup)

**Subject:** re: {same thread}

{One line that gives them an easy out: "if this is not a priority right now, no worries".}
{One line leaving the door open.}
